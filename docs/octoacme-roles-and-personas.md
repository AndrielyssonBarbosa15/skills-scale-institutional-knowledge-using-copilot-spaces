# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer workflows. They turn problem statements into clear user journeys, interface patterns, and interaction guidance that teams can implement confidently.

### Responsibilities
- Translate product requirements into user flows, wireframes, and interaction designs
- Validate design assumptions through lightweight research and usability feedback
- Define accessibility and usability expectations early in planning
- Partner with developers during implementation to maintain design quality
- Capture design decisions and rationale for future iterations

### Goals
- Improve usability and task completion for target users
- Reduce rework caused by unclear interaction requirements
- Ensure accessibility and consistency across user-facing features

### Typical Communication
- Regular collaboration with Product Managers on user needs and acceptance criteria
- Design handoff and implementation reviews with Developers
- Feedback loops with Stakeholders and Customer-facing roles after demos or releases

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts provide measurement frameworks and actionable insights that help teams prioritize effectively and evaluate project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics and baselines tied to project goals
- Build and maintain dashboards or reports for delivery and outcome tracking
- Analyze experiment and usage data to identify opportunities and risks
- Support backlog prioritization with evidence-based recommendations
- Highlight data quality issues that may affect decisions

### Goals
- Enable data-informed planning and trade-off decisions
- Improve visibility into delivery performance and customer outcomes
- Shorten the feedback loop between release and learning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metric reviews with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Insight sharing with Developers to guide iteration priorities
- Outcome summaries for Stakeholders during status updates and retrospectives

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers improve delivery reliability by automating build, test, deployment, and operational workflows across environments.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Define environment standards, release controls, and rollback readiness
- Partner on observability, alerting, and incident response readiness
- Improve infrastructure reliability, security, and operational documentation
- Support root-cause analysis and post-incident improvements

### Goals
- Increase deployment frequency with lower change failure rates
- Reduce manual release steps and operational risk
- Improve service stability and recovery time during incidents

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness planning with Project Managers and Developers
- Coordination with Developers on build failures, deployment issues, and runbook updates
- Incident and risk communication with Stakeholders when service impact occurs

---

## Customer Success Managers

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers represent post-release customer outcomes, ensuring onboarding, adoption, and feedback are continuously integrated into planning.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback from onboarding, support trends, and account reviews
- Surface adoption blockers and training needs to the delivery team
- Coordinate enablement materials and rollout communication for customer teams
- Partner with Product Managers on follow-up improvements and prioritization input
- Track customer health indicators related to delivered capabilities

### Goals
- Improve adoption and realized value from released features
- Reduce churn risk by addressing customer pain points early
- Strengthen the product feedback loop between customers and delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Customer insight handoffs to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Coordination with UX Designers and Developers on usability pain points
- Regular updates with Stakeholders on adoption trends and enablement needs
